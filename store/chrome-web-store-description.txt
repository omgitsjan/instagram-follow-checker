Instagram Follow Checker – relationships, charts & bots

Find mutual follows, people who don’t follow you back, and followers you don’t follow yet — plus local analytics charts and bot-style heuristics. Free, open source, and works with your normal Instagram login in Chrome. No Instagram API keys. No paid unlock. No data sent to our servers.


═══════════════════════════════════════
WHAT IT DOES
═══════════════════════════════════════

Instagram Follow Checker compares your Following list with your Followers list and organizes people into clear relationship views:

• Mutual – you follow them and they follow you
• Not following back – you follow them, they don’t follow you
• You don’t follow – they follow you, you don’t follow them

From the lists you can:
• Open any profile with one click
• Unfollow accounts that don’t follow you back
• Follow accounts that follow you (private accounts may show as “Requested”)
• Search by username
• Export results as JSON for your own records

v1.2 also adds:
• Analytics – doughnut/pie charts from your list buckets (mutual / not back / not you)
• Bots – local heuristic signals with optional actions
• Posts / Admire – marked coming soon

Profile pictures are shown when available. Your last analysis can be restored when you reopen the extension.


═══════════════════════════════════════
HOW IT WORKS (NO OFFICIAL API)
═══════════════════════════════════════

This extension does not use Instagram’s official Graph API and does not require Meta developer apps or tokens.

While you are logged into instagram.com in Chrome, it uses the same kind of browser session requests the Instagram website already makes to load follower/following lists. Comparison and display happen locally in your browser.

• No account password is collected by this extension
• No analysis data is uploaded to the developer’s servers
• Language preference and last results are stored only in Chrome’s local extension storage on your device


═══════════════════════════════════════
HOW TO USE
═══════════════════════════════════════

1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store
2. Open https://www.instagram.com and log in
3. Click the Instagram Follow Checker icon
4. Press “Start analysis” and wait for both lists to load
5. Browse the three tabs, search, export, or follow/unfollow as needed

Tips:
• Keep the Instagram tab open while analyzing
• Large accounts take longer (the extension pages results with short pauses to reduce rate limits)
• If Instagram returns an error or rate limit (e.g. 429), wait a few minutes and try again
• Use Settings (gear icon) to switch language English/Deutsch, open the source code, or tip the developer

PRIVACY
═══════════════════════════════════════

Permissions are limited to what the extension needs:
• Access to instagram.com (and related image CDNs) so it can read list data and show avatars while you are logged in
• Storage for language preference and the last local analysis result
• Active tab / scripting so the extension can talk to the open Instagram tab

We do not run a backend that receives your follower lists. Use of Instagram remains between you and Meta/Instagram under their policies.
